About AgroVI (AgroAI)

AgroVI is a pioneer in AI-driven smart agriculture, combining over three generations of field farming wisdom with edge computer vision, IoT telemetry, and automated fertigation systems.

Headquartered in the agricultural hub of Nashik, Maharashtra, AgroVI works directly with over 12,000+ farmers across India. Our goal is to eradicate crop loss caused by late pest detection, water wastage, and unoptimized soil fertilization.
